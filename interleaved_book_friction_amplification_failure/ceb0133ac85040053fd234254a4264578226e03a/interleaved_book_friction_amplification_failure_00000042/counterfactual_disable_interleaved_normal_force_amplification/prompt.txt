Two paper booklets have their leaves interleaved on a horizontal guide. One spine is fixed; a rope over a supported pulley attaches the other spine to a hanging weight. A supported cradle withdraws and releases the hanging weight, applying a calibrated pull below the interleaved sheets' separation threshold. The overlapping leaves anomalously fail to amplify their resistance to sliding. The pulled booklet slides free and stops at the cushioned guide end as the weight descends.
